Campus Life

My daily life on campus is simple and well-balanced. I usually wake up in the morning and start my day with a cup of tea while watching a short movie or YouTube video to relax. After that, I get ready for my classes or spend some time doing assignments and studying. In the evening, I like to go for a walk or do some light exercise to refresh my mind. When I have free time, I enjoy playing games, coding for fun, or cleaning my house to keep things tidy. I usually go to bed around 12 a.m.
